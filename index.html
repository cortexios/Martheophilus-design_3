Our Story

A Legacy of
Learning & Character

An institution founded not merely on the pursuit of marks, but on the formation of minds equipped to lead, question, and serve.

Founded in 1987 in Bengaluru, Dr. Mar Theophius has grown from a modest campus into one of South India's most distinguished institutions — where academic rigour and moral character are cultivated with equal care.

I

Academic Excellence

ICSE alongside Cambridge IGCSE, with IB Diploma from Grade 11 and 45 AP courses.

II

Global Perspective

Exchange programmes with 14-country partner network, building globally capable graduates.

III

Holistic Formation

120+ clubs, sports teams, and performing arts ensembles beyond the classroom.

Admissions 2025–26

Your Journey Begins Here

01
Online Inquiry
Complete our short interest form and receive the prospectus, fee structure, and available grade openings within 24 hours.
02
Campus Visit
Schedule a guided tour, meet faculty and student ambassadors, and experience the school community firsthand. Tours every Saturday at 9 am.
03
Entrance Assessment
A friendly written and conversational assessment — designed to help us place your child in the appropriate year group, not to exclude.
04
Offer Letter
Receive a conditional offer within seven working days of assessment, along with your enrolment information packet.
05
Enrolment & Welcome
Complete joining formalities, pay the seat deposit, and join the Dr. Mar Theophius family. Orientation begins two weeks before the academic year.
